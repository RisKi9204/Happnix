BOOKING POLICY

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective date: 18 November, 2026

1. Current availability

HAPPNIX is under development. No public ticket purchases or refunds are currently processed through this informational website.

2. Cancellation requests after launch

When booking is launched, the cancellation deadline and refund eligibility applicable to each event will be displayed before payment. Event-specific conditions may apply and will be disclosed before the customer confirms the booking.

3. Proposed HAPPNIX cancellation rule — confirmation required

The current project specification proposes that cancellations made more than 24 hours before an event may receive a refund after a 20% deduction, while cancellations within 24 hours may be non-refundable. This is a proposed rule only and must not be treated as active until the operator and event organiser approve it and it is displayed at checkout.

4. Event cancellation or material changes

If an event is cancelled or materially changed, customers will be informed using the contact details associated with their booking. The applicable refund or rescheduling process will be communicated in accordance with the event terms and applicable law.

5. Refund processing

Where a refund is approved, the expected processing timeline and destin ation payment method will be communicated to the customer. Actual bank or payment-provider timelines may vary.

6. Support
