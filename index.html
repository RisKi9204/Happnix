ABOUT US

Events deserve a simpler experience.

HAPPNIX is being developed to bring event discovery, organiser tools and ticket booking together in one place. The aim is to make it easier for people to find events and for organisers to manage event information and bookings.

HAPPNIX is currently under development. Features and availability may change before public launch.
